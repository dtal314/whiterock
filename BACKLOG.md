# WhiteRock backlog

Ordered by value to the questions the tool exists to answer.

1. **Executive-branch ingestion.** OGE 278e / 278-T PDFs for the President and cabinet are
   behind a search form. Build a polite crawler plus PDF parser, or keep the manual ledger
   and fill it from the published filings. Until then the site says so plainly.
2. **Legislation feed.** Congress.gov API (free key, stored as an Actions secret) for bills,
   markups and floor schedules, mapped with the same sector rules. Federal Register only
   covers executive and regulatory actions today.
3. **Sector rule review.** The agency, keyword and direction rules in
   `whiterock/mapping/universe.py` are transparent but hand-written. Review the "why" terms
   printed on the dashboard and tune false links.
4. **Paper filings.** OCR for scanned House and Senate PTRs (Tesseract) so the paper share
   stops being a blind spot.
5. **Model upgrades.** Per-ticker disclosure model (not just sector), amount-weighted labels,
   conformal intervals on the outperformance probabilities, and a public backtest page.
6. **Contract awards.** USAspending.gov award feed as a second kind of government action.
7. **Alerts.** Optional daily digest (email or RSS) generated by the Actions job.
8. **Look pass.** Dan's look-gate on the live site: colors, density, and the logo lockup.
