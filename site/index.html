Recommendations come from the pattern of disclosed congressional purchases, the direction of recent government actions, and the model probabilities shown elsewhere on this site. Each one is recorded with its date, entry price and model-portfolio amount, then tracked against its sector benchmark until it closes, so the record is public and cannot be rewritten. This is a research screen of public records, not personal investment advice.

WhiteRock is an open research tool. Data: Federal Register API, U.S. House Clerk financial disclosures, U.S. Senate eFD, unitedstates/congress-legislators, market prices via yfinance. Forecasts describe the likelihood of future public disclosures and of benchmark-relative price moves. They are not recommendations.
